Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Lead Cleaner & RevOps Pipeline ("we", "us", or "our") processes data collected through our HubSpot App Marketplace integration.

1. Data We Process

When you install and authorize our application, we access the following HubSpot CRM data via official OAuth 2.0 APIs solely to perform lead hygiene and deduplication:

  • Contact Details: Names, phone numbers, email addresses, cities, postal PIN codes, and GSTIN identifiers.
  • Company Associations: Company names and domain associations.
  • Portal Metadata: HubSpot Portal ID (hub_id) and domain used for multi-tenant account isolation.

2. How We Use Data

  • To standardize phone numbers to international Indian E.164 formats (+91).
  • To validate 6-digit postal PIN codes and verify GSTIN state codes.
  • To identify duplicate clusters and execute contact consolidation.
  • To calculate hygiene quality scores and executive audit summaries.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • No Persistent Contact Storing: Contact records processed in memory are never retained on our servers after execution.
  • Audit Logs: Aggregated execution metrics and anonymized field hashes are stored in an encrypted database for audit history.
  • Token Security: OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest and isolated by Portal ID. We never print, expose, or log customer tokens.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your CRM data. Data is only communicated back to your authorized HubSpot portal via official HubSpot APIs over TLS 1.3 encrypted HTTPS.

5. Data Deletion & Disconnection

When you uninstall the app from your HubSpot portal or click Disconnect in the app settings, your access tokens are invalidated immediately and tenant configurations are purged.

6. Contact Us

For questions regarding this policy or data processing, contact us at privacy@revopsbolt.in.
