HubSpot Marketplace Integration Setup

Lead Cleaner & RevOps Pipeline — Setup Guide

This guide walks you through connecting your HubSpot portal, configuring data governance rules, and standardizing contact records in under 10 minutes.

⚡ 10-Minute Quickstart Workflow

Step 1: Install & Authorize with HubSpot

From the HubSpot App Marketplace listing, click Install app. You will be prompted to select your HubSpot portal and grant required CRM object permissions (contacts, companies, deals). Once authorized, you will be redirected to the Lead Cleaner dashboard with your Portal ID automatically recognized.

Step 2: Choose Ingestion Source

Select your ingestion pipeline mode:

  • Vendor Spreadsheets (CSV / Excel): Upload offline trade expo, Meta Lead Ad, or WhatsApp lead files before importing to your CRM.
  • Direct CRM Hygiene: Stream live contacts directly from HubSpot via API to scan existing records.

Step 3: Run Hygiene & Pre-Flight Diagnostics

The normalization engine runs automated checks:

  • Standardizes 10-digit Indian phone numbers to E.164 (+91XXXXXXXXXX).
  • Validates 6-digit postal PIN codes and cross-checks GSTIN state codes.
  • Identifies duplicate clusters using RapidFuzz name/email matching.

Step 4: Non-Destructive Sync & Native Merges

By default, Dry-Run Safety Mode is active, simulating all API calls without live mutations. When ready, click Sync Patches to HubSpot or merge duplicates using HubSpot's native merge endpoint.

🛡️ Safety & Non-Destructive Policy

  • Never Overwrites Existing Valid Data: Valid properties in HubSpot are preserved unless explicit overwrite is enabled.
  • Gap-Fill Only: Missing phone numbers, postal codes, and city locations are enriched non-destructively.
  • Native HubSpot Merge: Contact merges preserve timeline history, email engagements, and deal associations.
